---
title: iOS
description: Embedded files and system time zone data.
---

Use `files()` with embedded locale files, as on [Android](/platforms/android): the app bundle is not where `dir`
looks. Time zones are read from the system (`/var/db/timezone/zoneinfo`, `/usr/share/zoneinfo`); in a Tauri app the
WebView's zone is the system zone. The start locale comes from the WebView's `navigator.languages`.

The protocol is `i18n-next://localhost` on iOS, as on macOS: the CSP needs `i18n-next:`
([Tauri](/guides/tauri#content-security-policy)).
